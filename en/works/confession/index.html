CONFESSION

“Confession begins where words come to an end”

material:birch burl, fire charring
dimensions:30 × 31 × 23 cm
weight:not specified
year:2026
status:AVAILABLE UPON REQUEST

The moment after everything has been spoken. Sins laid bare upon the table, exhaled into silence. But you lied. Even to yourself. What matters most constantly slips past language.

At the heart of the piece is an encounter with an uncompromising gaze. It does not judge. It simply leaves you no version of the story. You were seen long before you began to speak.

Exhibitions: 9th Annual Exhibition “Tryn*Trava. Modern Russian Style”
