RESENTMENT

material:birch burl, fire charring
dimensions:35 × 35 × 42 cm
weight:not specified
year:2026
status:AVAILABLE UPON REQUEST

At times, what lies between two beings is not a conversation but what remains after it: the unsaid, the wounded — what no longer has words. Two parts of the object grew from one tree and reach toward each other, but stay frozen, unable to close. A mute emptiness hangs between them. This is a work about distance and a dull silence that arises right before your eyes: a step toward each other has been made, but everything inside has turned to stone.

Exhibitions: 9th Annual Exhibition “Tryn*Trava. Modern Russian Style”
Moscow / Muravyov-Apostol Estate Museum, 2026
