FACE TO FACE

Wall sculpture / 2 elements

material: birch burl, fire charring
dimensions: 90 × 81 × 22 cm
weight: 5.66 kg
year: 2026
status: ON SALE FROM 2027 (stay tuned for announcements)

“Face to Face” — two times in one face

I belong to a generation that went, in a single lifetime, from playing with a stick in the yard to humanoid robots and artificial intelligence. Two ways of seeing meet in this work.

One looks back to the analog past: a lens from a film camera points to a childhood without smartphones or screens, when the street was the main place to play and time was measured by a football in the yard. The other looks to the digital present: a lens from a digital camera, from a world where gadgets have become part of everyday life and artificial intelligence is rapidly changing reality itself.

Both lenses are set into the natural forms of birch burl and look almost identical. Behind this likeness are two different experiences, two ways of seeing.

One generation, one person, one face
