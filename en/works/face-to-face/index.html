FACE TO FACE

Wall sculpture / 2 elements

material:birch burl, fire charring
dimensions:90 × 81 × 22 cm
weight:5.66 kg
year:2026
status:ON SALE FROM 01.01.2027

In this work, the eye functions not merely as an organ of sight, but as an instrument of observation.

Camera lenses replace biological pupils: the left eye features a vintage Soviet “Vilia” lens rooted in childhood nostalgia, while the right holds a contemporary digital lens. Two gazes separated by time exist within a single face — analogue memory meeting digital reality.
