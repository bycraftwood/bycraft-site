THE WOLF

material:birch burl, fire charring
dimensions:45 × 36 × 19 cm
weight:3.3 kg
year:2026
status:AVAILABLE UPON REQUEST

The image of the wolf emerges from within the natural form of the burl rather than being applied from outside — there is nothing to guess at. This is a work about character: no aggression, but the heavy calm and wariness of an animal that lets no one come too close.

Exhibitions: 9th Annual Exhibition “Tryn*Trava. Modern Russian Style”
Moscow / Muravyov-Apostol Estate Museum, 2026
